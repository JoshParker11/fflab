## Chase Brown: sell while the market still likes him

- Samaje Perine took **50% of the carries** in Week 5 to Brown's 46%. Brown has hit double digits in only 2 of 5 games, and experts have him sliding from RB2 to RB3/flex.
- His **market value is still 4.5k**, roughly RB1/2 money. That gap is your selling window. One more bad week after the bye and it closes.
- Projections still like his playoff schedule (CAR, IND, BAL ≈ 15–17), which is why the model doesn't say "dump him." Swap him for something *more certain*, not for less.

**James Cook** is Buffalo's workhorse (24–25 carries in his big games), projected 16–19 every week. His playoff opponents are CHI, DEN and MIA. mgcoluccio is 3-2 but has only an 8% playoff chance by the sims (Jayden Daniels' elbow). They're RB-rich (Swift, Braelon Allen, Wright, Sampson) and starting Kincaid at TE, which is why Brown + Fannin *helps* their lineup. **Pitch:** "You've got five running backs and need a TE. I'll give you an RB1-type plus a starting TE for Cook." If they push back, add Godwin or Bernard (both near-zero value to you). Make the offer **this weekend**. Brown's price only holds while his bye week hides the Perine split, and one more 50/50 game after the bye would cut it.

**DJ Moore:** your read is reasonable. It's a 2-for-1 with two players who add nothing to your playoff lineup, so it costs you almost nothing. But the upside is modest. He projects 12–15 once healthy, and in Weeks 16–17 he'd be your 7th–8th option behind Strange. His AC-joint sprain has flared twice, he missed Week 5 and hasn't practiced this week, and Buffalo's Week 7 bye means Week 8 at the earliest. Treat it as consolidation plus a lottery ticket, not a needle-mover. If you do the Cook deal with Fannin, offer **Godwin + B. Robinson** for Moore instead.

**Maye:** Footballguys has him at QB3 after a monster two weeks, so you'd be buying at the top. More importantly, Dak's Weeks 16–17 matchups (JAX, NYG) project about as well as Maye's (NYJ, DEN). Maye mostly helps the regular season you've already won. Pass, unless tbwelch will take Fannin-level value.

## Bench and roster plan

Your bench today adds almost nothing to Weeks 16–17 (Fannin, Godwin, Bernard, B. Robinson ≈ 0 each). Use those spots in this order:

1. **Consolidate** two of them in the Cook and/or DJ Moore deals.
2. **Charbonnet:** Seattle must activate him from PUP by **Oct 22** (Week 7 vs KC looks likely). When he's activated, he leaves the IR slot and needs a roster spot. Cut B. Robinson if he's still around.
3. **Keep Strange.** He's your TE insurance behind LaPorta and a 12–13 point flex in the playoffs. Fannin is the TE to move. Three TEs is one too many once the Lions are back.
4. **Bye-week map:** W7 Strange · W9 Bernard · W10 Irving, Godwin · W11 Watson, Fannin, Charbonnet · W13 Flowers, Shrader · W14 **Dak + Javonte** (stream a QB that week; it's the last regular-season week and seeding is likely settled).
5. **Weeks 12–14:** use the open spots to grab a **defense and kicker with good Week 16–17 matchups**, plus one **backup RB** behind a starter on a run-heavy team. Injuries in December are what actually decide titles.

## Trade deadline: Week 11
You have about five weeks. Do the Brown sale now (before another 50/50 game drags his price down). After that, only make moves that improve the **Weeks 16–17 lineup or its injury cover**. You're right that a lot comes down to chance. With a 98% chance at the bye, the variance you can still control is depth in December.
